Tax and legal

Last updated 19 November 2026. This page explains the basics in plain language. The creator terms you accept in your dashboard are the agreement itself; if the two ever differ, the terms apply. Nothing here is tax or legal advice for your own situation.

1. You work for yourself

As a Mindroll creator you are an independent creator, not an employee, agent or partner of Mindroll. You choose what to post, when and where. Mindroll does not provide wages, benefits, insurance or equipment, and you cannot sign anything or make promises on Mindroll's behalf.

You must be 18 or older, or the age at which you can enter a contract where you live.

2. Taxes

  • What you earn here is income. You are responsible for reporting it and paying any tax you owe where you live. Mindroll does not withhold tax from your payouts unless the law requires it.
  • Before a payout, we may ask you for tax details. In the United States that is usually a Form W-9; outside the United States it is usually a Form W-8BEN. If we need one and do not have it, your payout waits until we do.
  • Where the law requires it, we report what we paid you to the tax authority and send you a copy (in the United States, a Form 1099).
  • Keep your own record of what you were paid. Your wallet shows every payment.

3. Say that you are paid

You earn money when people subscribe through your link, so every post that promotes Mindroll must say so clearly. This is the law in the United States, the United Kingdom, the European Union and most other places, and it is also a rule on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

  • Use the platform's paid partnership label where there is one, or put #ad or #MindrollPartner where people see it without tapping "more".
  • Only say things about Mindroll that are true and that you believe. Do not promise results the app cannot guarantee.

4. Your content

  • Your videos stay yours.
  • You allow Mindroll to repost, share and feature the videos you make for the program, including in our own channels and ads, with credit to you where the format allows.
  • Only post what you have the right to post: your own footage, and music and clips the platform licenses for commercial use.
  • No hateful, sexual, dangerous or misleading content, and nothing that pretends to be Mindroll itself.

5. How pay works

  • You earn commission on subscriptions that come through your link, plus the bonuses and prizes shown in your dashboard. The rates and rules shown there are the ones that apply, and we can change them for future earnings.
  • Earnings clear after a waiting period, shown in your wallet, so that refunds can be taken into account. If a subscriber is refunded or charges back, the commission on that sale is reversed.
  • Payouts are sent to the payout method you give us, once you reach the minimum shown in your wallet. Make sure the details are yours and correct; we cannot recover money sent to details you entered wrongly.
  • Bonuses and prizes are paid only for real activity that follows these rules.

6. Fair play

No bought or automated views, no fake accounts or fake sign-ups, no spam, and no misleading people into subscribing. If we suspect any of this we can hold your payouts while we check, withdraw what was earned that way, and remove you from the program.

7. Leaving or being removed

You can leave at any time by emailing us. We can pause or end your place in the program, for example for breaking these rules. When that happens your link stops earning, and money you earned fairly before then is still paid out.

8. Your information

To run the program we keep your name, email, the social accounts you link, your payout details, and the public numbers on the videos you post (views, likes and so on). We use them to count your earnings, pay you, and meet our tax and legal duties. See the privacy policy for the rest.

9. Questions

Email teammindroll@gmail.com. For advice about your own taxes, ask an accountant or your local tax authority.
