4. What Mindroll is — and what it is not

Mindroll gives you short written and narrated ideas drawn from books, essays, talks and research, along with tools to save, review and revisit them.

Two things to be clear about:

They are not financial, medical, legal or psychological advice, and you should not treat them as a substitute for a professional who knows your circumstances.
